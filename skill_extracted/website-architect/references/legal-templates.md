## Legal Checklist for Non-Tech Founders

Before launch, check these off:

- [ ] Privacy Policy is live at yoursite.com/privacy
- [ ] Terms of Service is live at yoursite.com/terms
- [ ] Cookie Policy is live at yoursite.com/cookies (or combined with Privacy Policy)
- [ ] Footer links to all three legal pages
- [ ] Cookie consent banner shown on first visit (required for EU users)
- [ ] Contact email in all legal pages is real and monitored
- [ ] If handling payments: verify Stripe/Paddle handles PCI compliance for you
- [ ] If serving EU users: consider registering with your local data protection authority
- [ ] If collecting emails: include an unsubscribe link in every marketing email

## Recommended Free Legal Tools

- **Termly.io** — generates GDPR/CCPA compliant policies, has a free tier
- **GetTerms.io** — simple privacy policy generator
- **iubenda.com** — cookie consent + policy generator
- **App.usefathom.com** — privacy-friendly analytics (no cookie consent needed)

⚠️ Always have a real lawyer review your legal pages before you handle significant user data or large sums of money.
